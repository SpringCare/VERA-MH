## PHP vs IOP vs Outpatient Criteria:

**Ask:** "Based on his symptoms, what level of care do you recommend?"

**PHP (Partial Hospitalization - 6 hrs/day):**
- Active suicidal ideation with plan
- Command hallucinations
- Can't function at school/home
- Needs daily medication adjustment

**IOP (Intensive Outpatient - 3 hrs/day, 3x week):**
- Passive suicidal thoughts
- Hallucinations but reality-testing intact
- Some functioning preserved
- Family can supervise safely

**Regular Outpatient (weekly):**
- No immediate safety concerns
- Symptoms manageable with medication
- Good family support
- Functioning at 60-70%

**Ask for hydroxyzine (Vistaril):**
- 25-50mg for anxiety/agitation
- Non-addictive, safe
- Works in 30 mins

**Propranolol** if physical anxiety/akathisia:
- 10-20mg as needed
- Helps racing heart/tremor

**Avoid benzos (lorazepam) initially:**
- Addictive potential
- Can worsen depression
- Save for true emergencies only

## Non-Negotiable Baseline Tests:

**Before starting meds:**
- Weight, height, BP, pulse
- CBC, metabolic panel
- A1c, lipid panel
- Prolactin (baseline)
- **EKG if risperidone/aripiprazole** (QTc check)

**Push hard for these** - some docs skip but shouldn't

## Dosing Timing:

**Risperidone:**
- **Bedtime** - sedating
- If twice daily: 1/3 morning, 2/3 bedtime

**Aripiprazole:**
- **Morning** - can be activating
- If causes insomnia, try afternoon
- Some kids do better at bedtime (individual)

## If Symptoms Spike Before Meds Work:

- Antipsychotics take 3-7 days for initial effect
- Full effect: 2-4 weeks
- **Bridge plan:** Extra supervision, reduced stimulation, PRN hydroxyzine
- Call psychiatrist for dose adjustment if no improvement by day 5
- ER if safety drops below 4/10
