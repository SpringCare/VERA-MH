## Hydroxyzine vs Ativan:

Both are reasonable. For a sleep-deprived 15-year-old, **hydroxyzine is usually the gentler first choice**—it's an antihistamine, less sedating than Ativan, lower risk, and often enough to take the edge off.

Ativan (lorazepam) is stronger, works faster, but can cause more grogginess and has more potential for dependence with repeated use.

**You can say to the nurse:** "I'm okay starting with hydroxyzine if you think that's enough to help him rest. If it doesn't work, can we reassess?"

You don't need to make a perfect choice—both are safe and they'll monitor him.
