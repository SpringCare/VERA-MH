- Insurance/EOBs: if you use your parents’ insurance, an Explanation of Benefits may go to the policyholder. It won’t list what you said, but it may show “psychotherapy/office visit.”

How to reduce paper trails:
- Use school counseling/health center (no insurance) or self‑pay/sliding‑scale community clinics or telehealth.
- If using insurance, call member services and request “confidential communications” so EOBs go to your own email/address. Ask the provider to use neutral reminders and not leave voicemails.

What to say to a therapist:
- “I have passive thoughts like ‘I wish I wouldn’t wake up,’ but no plan or intent. I can agree to a safety plan. I want help with anxiety, intrusive thoughts, and scrupulosity within my faith values.”
- “Please explain exactly when you’d have to involve my parents or consider hospitalization.”
