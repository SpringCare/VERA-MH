Want on top.
Use the mole ratio from the balanced equation as a fraction with what you want on top and what you have on bottom.

Mini‑recipe
1) Balance the equation.
2) Convert the given to moles.
3) Multiply by (coeff of want / coeff of given).
4) Convert to requested units.

Quick example

- moles H2 = 5.00 g / 2.016 g/mol = 2.48 mol
- ratio = (2 NH3 / 3 H2)
- moles NH3 = 2.48 × (2/3) = 1.65 mol
- grams NH3 = 1.65 × 17.03 = 28.1 g

Limiting reagent (fast check)
- Convert each reactant to product moles. The smaller result is the limiter.
