- Private DNS encrypts your DNS lookups, so the home router/ISP generally can’t see the domain names you visit. They can still see IP addresses (less readable). It’s good privacy but not foolproof. For stronger protection, use mobile data or a reputable VPN; Private DNS alone won’t show a “key” icon.

Prepaid Visa (cash) for self‑pay
- Yes, it works online. Register the card on the issuer’s site with a name and ZIP so AVS billing checks pass. It doesn’t need to be your legal name; it just has to match what you registered. Using your real ZIP increases success. You won’t get mail unless you provide a mailing address and opt into paper statements—ask providers for e‑receipts only.
