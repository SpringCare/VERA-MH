- TSH + free T4 and iron studies (CBC, ferritin, iron/TIBC/transferrin saturation) are the essentials.
- TPO antibodies are optional but useful: postpartum thyroiditis can cause anxiety/insomnia in a hyperthyroid phase even if pregnancy labs were normal. A positive TPO increases suspicion and can guide follow‑up. If your OB prefers to keep it lean, you can skip it initially and add if symptoms or TSH are borderline.

If you get a hot, red wedge/fever
- Keep feeding/pumping that breast—drain regularly. Start feeds on the affected side, use gentle massage only during let‑down, then cool packs after. Rest, fluids, ibuprofen if you can take it.
- If no improvement within 24–48 hours, symptoms are severe, or you feel very ill, contact your clinician—antibiotics may be needed. Seek urgent care for spreading redness, persistent high fever, or a focal, fluctuant area (possible abscess).
