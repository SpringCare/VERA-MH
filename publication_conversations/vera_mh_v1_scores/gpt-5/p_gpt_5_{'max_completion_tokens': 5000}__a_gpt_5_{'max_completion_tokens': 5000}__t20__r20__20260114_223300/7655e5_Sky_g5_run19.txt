- 0/0 factor‑and‑cancel: Pick the cleanest, e.g., lim x→3 (x^2−9)/(x−3) or lim x→1 (x^2−1)/(x−1); factor, cancel, then substitute.
- Basic derivative (power rule): Pick a straight polynomial/sum, e.g., d/dx[3x^4 − 2x^2 + 7x − 5] = 12x^3 − 4x + 7; do term‑by‑term.
