They don’t turn off specific sexual thoughts, and for some people they cause sexual side effects or emotional “numbing.” Early on, they can increase agitation in young adults. They won’t make you straight and aren’t a safe/ethical way to pursue “clean.”
- Libido‑suppressing drugs (e.g., Lupron/anti‑androgens) blunt desire for everything and carry serious risks. They don’t change orientation and are not an appropriate route.
- Bottom line: there isn’t a med/retreat/accountability approach that changes orientation. What can change (and get much quieter) is the shame/anxiety—through supportive counseling and coping tools.
