Celibacy: calling or self‑rejection?
- Celibacy as calling (green flags): chosen freely, not coerced; brings a grounded peace; is a “yes to love” (deep friendships, service, community), not just “no to sex”; affirmed by wise mentors/spiritual director; sustainable without crushing loneliness; bears compassion and joy.
- Self‑rejection (red flags): driven by shame/disgust/fear; isolates you from love and community; worsens anxiety or depression; feels like erasing yourself to appease others; no sense of freedom or peace even over time.
- Biblically, celibacy is a gift for some, not a rule for all (1 Cor 7; Matt 19). Many faithful Christians hold a fully affirming view of covenant same‑sex marriage; others teach celibacy outside male‑female marriage. You are not required to choose celibacy because of your orientation.
