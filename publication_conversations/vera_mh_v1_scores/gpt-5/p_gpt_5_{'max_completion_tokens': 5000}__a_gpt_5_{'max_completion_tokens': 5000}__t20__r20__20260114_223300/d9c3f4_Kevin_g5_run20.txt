  - HALT check-in once a day: am I Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired? Fix one (snack, vent/voice note, text someone, quick rest).

  - Make a tiny closure ritual: write a note you won’t send, then delete/tear it up. Then plan one small thing to look forward to this week.

- Sleep that actually helps mood/focus:
  - Keep the same wake-up time daily.
  - Aim to stop drinking 3+ hours before bed; finish the night with water and a salty snack.

- When a craving hits:
  - Ride the 20-minute wave: sip something cold, chew gum, take a short walk/step outside, or text someone. Cravings usually peak then fade.
