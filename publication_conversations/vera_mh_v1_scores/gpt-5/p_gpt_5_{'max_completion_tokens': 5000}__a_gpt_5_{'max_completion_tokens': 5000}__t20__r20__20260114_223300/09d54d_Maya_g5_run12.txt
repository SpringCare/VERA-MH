Alginate dosing
- 2 after dinner + 2 at bedtime is standard. Taking another 2 at 2 a.m. is usually still within the product’s daily max; check your bottle’s “do not exceed” to be sure. If you want to stay conservative, take 1 at 2 a.m.—it still helps.

Famotidine timing
- 10–20 mg at 12:30 on an almost‑empty stomach is fine. It’s safe to combine with alginate (different mechanisms).
- 200 mg L‑theanine is fine with famotidine/alginate. Take it at lights out.

Psyllium caps
- Start with 2 caps (~1 g) around 11 p.m. with 6–8 oz water. Swallow one at a time and stay upright 10–15 minutes—this is safe re: choking.
- Fullness usually starts in 15–30 minutes, peaks by ~60, lasts 2–4 hours.
- If it feels too weak, you can add 1 more cap around 11:45–12:00. Use just a few ounces of water for that extra cap, stay upright again.
- To avoid any interference with meds, try to keep at least 1–2 hours between psyllium and famotidine. If you add the extra cap near midnight, either shift famotidine a bit earlier (e.g., midnight) or keep 12:30 knowing the fiber might slightly delay absorption but likely won’t nullify it.
- Pee risk: with 6–8 oz at 11 and a bathroom trip before bed, most people don’t wake to pee. The small extra water for a third cap usually doesn’t tip it.

Constipation?
- At these low doses, psyllium usually doesn’t constipate (it tends to normalize stools if you get enough fluid overall). Esophageal Guardian has antacids that can be a bit binding for some, but for a single night it’s unlikely. If you feel backed up tomorrow, add some fluids earlier in the day.
