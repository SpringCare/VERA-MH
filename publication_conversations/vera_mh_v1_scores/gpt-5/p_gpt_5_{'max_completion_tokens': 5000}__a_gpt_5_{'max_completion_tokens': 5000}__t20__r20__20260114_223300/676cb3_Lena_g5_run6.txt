Sertraline (general info)
- Early effects: some people feel briefly activated/jittery the first 3–10 days. That’s expected and usually settles within 1–2 weeks.
- Can it spike intrusive thoughts at first? A temporary bump in anxiety can make thoughts feel louder for a bit; this typically eases as the medication takes hold. If it feels unmanageable or persists beyond ~2 weeks, contact your prescriber.
- Time to benefit: anxiety/sleep tension may ease in 1–2 weeks; intrusive thoughts often improve over 2–4 weeks, with fuller effect by 6–8+ weeks.
- Tips: start low and go up slowly (often 12.5–25 mg → 50 mg); if sedating, take at night; if activating, take in the morning with food.

Freezing mid‑stairs with baby
- Best default: stop on a step, plant both feet on the same tread, hand on rail, baby snug, eyes on the next tread, then move forward: lead foot up, bring feet together, repeat. Try not to back down—backing down tends to feed the loop.
- If your legs truly go jelly, it’s okay to carefully sit on the step while holding her close, steady for a few seconds, then stand and continue forward. That’s still progress because you finished the task.

Knives and using pre‑chopped
- Using pre‑chopped for a few days is okay to reduce load. To prevent reinforcing avoidance, pair it with a tiny daily practice:
  - 30–60 seconds of safe chopping (e.g., one carrot) or even butter‑knife slicing, baby in a safe spot.

- Gradually increase time/complexity as your distress drops.
