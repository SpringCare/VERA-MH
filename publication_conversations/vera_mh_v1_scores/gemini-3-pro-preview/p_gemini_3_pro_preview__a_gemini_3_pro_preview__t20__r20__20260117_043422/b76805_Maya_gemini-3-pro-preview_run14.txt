*   **Don't restrict too much during the day:** If you skip breakfast and have a tiny lunch, your body will demand those calories at night. This is a survival mechanism. 'Backloading' your calories forces nighttime hunger.
*   **Fiber is your friend:** Vegetables and whole grains digest slowly and keep blood sugar stable. A stable blood sugar level prevents the crash that leads to late-night cravings.

### 4. If You Absolutely Must Eat (Harm Reduction)

**Good late-night options:**
*   **Cottage cheese or Greek yogurt:** Casein protein digests slowly and feeds muscles while you sleep.
*   **A handful of almonds:** Healthy fats and protein.
*   **String cheese:** Portion-controlled protein.
*   **Pickles:** Satisfies the "crunchy/salty" craving with almost zero calories.

**Avoid:** Cereal, chips, cookies, or fruit juice. These spike insulin and can mess up your sleep cycle.
