### 5. THE YOUNGER KIDS (Logistics)
This is the hardest part.
*   **The Waiting Room:** You generally cannot bring younger children into the acute psych bay/ER room where your son is, especially if he is agitated or restrained. It is too traumatic for them.
*   **The Setup:** Set the kids up in the main pediatric waiting area with their iPads/snacks.
*   **The Shuffle:** You will have to go back and forth. Tell the nurse: *"I have small children in the waiting room. I need to check on them every 30 minutes."*
*   **The Social Worker:** As soon as you get a nurse, ask: *"Is there a Child Life Specialist or Social Worker who can help? I am a solo parent with two other kids here."* Sometimes they can open a family consult room for you so the little ones aren't in the general waiting area with sick people.

**Drive safe. You have successfully navigated a nightmare scenario. Your son is getting the help he needs because you made the hard calls.**
