    *   Doom scroll, answer that last Slack, check the scores.
    *   Then, **change the audio.** Switch from "Tech News" or aggressive music to something acoustic or chill.
    *   Take 3 deep breaths.
    *   *Then* drive the last block.
*   **The "Shower Reset":** This is the best hack. Bring a change of clothes in a gym bag. When you walk in, say: *"Babe, work was a dumpster fire and I feel gross. Can I hop in your shower for 5 minutes to wash the day off so I can actually focus on you?"*
    *   **Why this works:** The temperature change (hot water) physically signals your nervous system to switch gears. Plus, you come out smelling good and wearing "chill" clothes. It’s a hard reset.

### 2. Solo "Phone Bowl" (Beating the 5-Minute Urge)
You can’t rely on honor. You need friction.
*   **The "Charging Station" Rule:** Buy a cheap 6-foot charging cable. Plug it in behind your couch or in a corner **hard to reach** from where you sit.
    *   **The Rule:** If the phone is charging, you have to stand up to use it. You can play music/podcasts via Bluetooth speaker, but if you want to scroll, you have to stand in the corner like a punished child. Your laziness will usually beat your boredom.
*   **The "Time Lock" Safe:** If you have $50, buy a "Kitchen Safe" (It’s a plastic box with a timer). You lock the phone in for 1 hour. There is no override code. You have to smash it with a hammer to open it early. It’s extremely effective for ADHD.

### 3. Gym: Seated HIIT (Dumbbells & Cables Only)
Since you don't have ropes, we need to replicate that "constant tension" fatigue.

**The "Shoulder Smoke" Circuit:**
*Set a timer: 40 seconds work / 20 seconds rest. 4 Rounds.*

1.  **Seated DB "Speed" Press:** Grab light dumbbells (like 15-20lbs). Press them overhead as fast as you can with good form. Don't lock out at the top, keep the tension. Just pump.
2.  **Cable "Face Pulls" (High Reps):** Sit at a cable row station or pull a bench to a cable tower. Use the rope attachment. Pull to your forehead, spreading the ropes. Focus on speed and squeezing your rear delts.
3.  **Seated "Bus Drivers":** Hold a weight plate (25lb or 35lb) or a single dumbbell straight out in front of you with straight arms. Twist it left and right like a steering wheel. This torches your core and shoulders.
4.  **Seated Shadow Boxing:** (With 3-5lb weights). Punch straight out. Fast hands.

*Total time: ~12 minutes.* You will be dripping sweat and your arms will be shaking too much to text.

### 5. Explaining "Emotionally Distant" (Without Sounding Defensive)
This is tricky. If you say "It's my ADHD," it sounds like an excuse. If you say nothing, it happens again.

**The Script for the Future (Ownership + Strategy):**
Don't talk about *intent* ("I didn't mean to"). Talk about *impact* and *prevention*.

*   *"I’ve learned that I have a really hard time switching off 'Work Mode.' In the past, I’d come home and basically be a zombie because my battery was dead, which made my partner feel ignored. I don't want to do that again. So if I ask for 20 minutes of quiet when I first see you, or if I put my phone in the other room, that’s me trying to make sure I have actual energy for you."*

**Why this works:**
1.  You admit the flaw ("I become a zombie").
2.  You validate the impact ("It made them feel ignored").
3.  You present a solution ("I take 20 mins / hide the phone").

It shows you are self-aware and working on it, rather than just saying "Take me as I am."
