**2. Caffeine Cutoff**
**Yes, the noon cutoff is mandatory.**
*   **The Math:** Caffeine has a half-life of ~5 hours. If you drink a coffee at 4 PM, half of it is still in your blood at 9 PM.
*   **The 3 AM Link:** Even if you fall asleep fine, that residual caffeine prevents you from entering "Deep Sleep." That makes your sleep fragile, so when your sleep cycle shifts at 3 AM, you wake up fully instead of rolling over.
*   **Rule:** No caffeine after 12:00 PM. Switch to decaf or water. It will suck for 2 days, then your sleep will transform.
