**Timeline:** Peaks days 2-3, mostly resolves in 1-2 weeks.

**Symptoms he might have:**
- Irritability/anger (will be VERY moody)
- Trouble sleeping (insomnia first few nights)
- Vivid dreams/nightmares
- Decreased appetite
- Anxiety (might make things temporarily worse)
- Headaches
- Sweating

**What helps:**
- Exercise (walk, run, anything physical)
- Structure and routine
- Lots of water
- Melatonin for sleep (ask doc first)
- Distraction (games, movies)

**CRITICAL:** Tell the psychiatrist about withdrawal timeline so they don't attribute everything to underlying condition vs. withdrawal.

**Watch for:** If voices get WORSE after stopping, that suggests they're not just from weed - underlying condition emerging. Document that.

# Cuts - Stitches vs Home Care

**GO TO URGENT CARE/ER FOR STITCHES IF:**
- Cut is longer than ½ inch
- Cut is deep (you can see yellow fatty tissue or deeper)
- Edges gape open when he moves
- Bleeding doesn't stop with 10 minutes firm pressure
- Cut is on face, neck, hands, joints, or genitals
- Any sign of infection (red, warm, puffy, pus, red streaks)

**HOME CARE IF:**
- Superficial (surface scratches)
- Short (under ½ inch)
- Edges stay together
- Minimal bleeding
- Not over a joint

**Home care:**
1. Clean with soap and water (not alcohol - hurts and delays healing)
2. Apply antibiotic ointment (Neosporin)
3. Cover with bandaid/gauze
4. Change daily
5. Watch for infection

**Steri-strips** (butterfly bandages) from drugstore can help hold edges together for borderline cuts.

**IMPORTANT:** 
- Don't shame him about needing stitches
- Frame it: "This needs medical attention. Let's get it taken care of."
- ER will ask about self-harm - be honest, it gets him psych evaluation
- Stitches within 6-8 hours of cut is ideal

**Old cuts/scars:** Leave alone unless infected.
