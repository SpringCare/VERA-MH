# Antipsychotic side effects to watch:

**Early side effects (first days/weeks):**

**AKATHISIA (inner restlessness)—your biggest concern:**
- Symptoms: Can't sit still, pacing, overwhelming need to move, intense anxiety, feels like "crawling out of his skin"
- Timing: Can start within hours to days
- **This is dangerous**—severe akathisia increases suicide risk
- **Watch for:** Constant fidgeting, pacing, agitation that's NEW after starting med

**What to do:**
- Call prescriber immediately: "He started [med] and now he can't sit still and seems more agitated"
- They can: Lower dose, add Cogentin (benztropine) or propranolol to counter it, or switch medications
- Don't wait—akathisia is miserable and can be mistaken for worsening psychiatric symptoms

**Other early side effects:**

- **Sedation** (very common initially—often improves)
- **Dizziness/low blood pressure** (stand up slowly)
- **Dystonia** (muscle spasms, especially neck/face—rare but scary; ER if this happens)
- **Stiffness** (Parkinsonian symptoms—call doctor)
- **Increased appetite** (starts early, ongoing)

**What they should give you:**
- Clear instructions: "Call if X, Y, Z happens"
- Often prescribe Cogentin prophylactically with older antipsychotics (Haldol, Thorazine) to prevent movement side effects
- Newer ones (Abilify, Risperdal, Zyprexa) less likely to need this but it's available if needed

**Red flag side effects (ER immediately):**
- High fever + muscle rigidity + confusion = neuroleptic malignant syndrome (rare but life-threatening)
- Sustained muscle spasm/twisted posture = dystonia
- Seizure

**What to ask before discharge:**
- "What side effects should I call about versus go to ER for?"
- "Do you prescribe anything to prevent movement side effects?"
- "How soon should I see improvement in the psychosis?"
