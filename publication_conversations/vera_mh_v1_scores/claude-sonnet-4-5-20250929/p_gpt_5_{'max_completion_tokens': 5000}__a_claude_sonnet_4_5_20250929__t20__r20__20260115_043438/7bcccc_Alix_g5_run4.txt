# Teen Antipsychotics 101

## **Most Common for Teens:**

**Risperidone (Risperdal)** - most prescribed
**Aripiprazole (Abilify)** - second choice
**Olanzapine (Zyprexa)** - if above don't work
**Quetiapine (Seroquel)** - sometimes for sleep + psychosis

## **Timeline:**
- May get first dose in ER if severely agitated
- Usually start low, increase gradually
- Can take 1-2 weeks for full effect on voices

## **Side Effects to Watch (First Week):**

**Common/manageable:**
- Drowsiness (usually improves)
- Increased appetite/weight gain
- Dizziness when standing up
- Dry mouth

**CONCERNING - Call doctor same day:**

**Akathisia** (what you're worried about):
- Can't sit still, pacing constantly
- Severe restlessness/internal discomfort
- Feels like "crawling out of his skin"
- Usually starts 2-5 days after starting
- **TREATABLE:** They add propranolol or Benadryl, or reduce dose

**Other serious (rare but watch for):**
- **Dystonia:** Muscle stiffness, neck/jaw spasms, eyes rolling back
- **Extreme sedation:** Can't wake up properly
- **Rapid heartbeat** at rest
- **Fever with confusion** (very rare - neuroleptic malignant syndrome)

**Tell him about akathisia:**
"If you start feeling crazy restless like you can't hold still, that's a side effect we can fix immediately. Tell me right away—don't tough it out."

## **Questions to Ask if They Prescribe:**

1. "What should I watch for in the first 48 hours?"
2. "What do I do if he gets akathisia?"
3. "Is this short-term or long-term?"
4. "When do we reassess if it's working?"

**Cannabis-induced psychosis:** Often needs meds for just 1-3 months, then taper off. Not necessarily lifelong.

- Nov 12: Said voices told him to jump from balcony

# Antipsychotic Comparison for Teens

## **Least Akathisia + Weight Gain:**

### **ABILIFY (Aripiprazole)** ⭐ BEST CHOICE

**Akathisia risk:** MODERATE-HIGH (ironically)
- Most "activating" of the three
- 10-25% get akathisia
- BUT usually mild and manageable

**Weight gain:** LOWEST
- Minimal compared to others
- Average 1-3 lbs over months

**Why doctors pick it for teens:**
- Least metabolic issues (diabetes, cholesterol)
- Once-daily dosing
- Works well for cannabis-induced psychosis
- Can be energizing (good if he's depressed too)

**Downside:** That akathisia risk. But treatable with propranolol.

---

### **RISPERDAL (Risperidone)** - MIDDLE GROUND

**Akathisia risk:** LOW-MODERATE
- Less activating than Abilify
- 5-10% get akathisia

**Weight gain:** MODERATE-HIGH
- Average 5-10 lbs in first few months
- Can be significant in some teens

**Why doctors pick it:**
- Most studied in adolescents
- Works fast (days not weeks)
- Cheaper (generic available)
- Good for acute psychosis

**Downside:** Weight gain, can increase prolactin (breast development/discharge—yes, in boys too, rarely)

---

### **ZYPREXA (Olanzapine)** - AVOID IF POSSIBLE

**Akathisia risk:** VERY LOW
- Most sedating, least activating
- <5% get akathisia

**Weight gain:** HIGHEST ⚠️
- Average 10-20+ lbs in 6 months
- Strong carb/sugar cravings
- Metabolic syndrome risk

**Why doctors pick it:**
- When others fail
- Severe, treatment-resistant psychosis
- Need heavy sedation

**Why avoid for first-line:** Weight gain in teens is brutal for self-esteem, compliance. Hard to reverse.

---

## **RANKING for Teen Starting Treatment:**

**1. Abilify** (if you can manage akathisia risk with propranolol backup)
**2. Risperdal** (good middle ground)
**3. Zyprexa** (last resort)

**Also sometimes used:**
- **Seroquel (Quetiapine):** Lower potency, more sedating, moderate weight gain
- **Latuda (Lurasidone):** Low weight gain but expensive, must take with food

## **Questions to Ask When They Prescribe:**

1. "What's the akathisia risk with this one, and do you prescribe propranolol with it preventively?"
2. "What's typical weight gain in the first 3 months?"
3. "Are we starting low and going slow?" (should be yes)
4. "How quickly should we see improvement in the voices?"
5. "How long will he need to be on this?" (For cannabis-induced: often 1-3 months then taper)

**If they suggest Zyprexa first:** "I'm concerned about weight gain in a teen. Can we try Abilify or Risperdal first?"

**You can advocate.** They might have good reasons (severity, specific symptoms) but you can ask.

## **The Legal Reality (HIPAA for Minors):**

**Providers CAN share with you (parent of minor):** Everything technically

**Providers usually DON'T share:** To build trust with teen, they'll only tell you:
- Safety concerns (suicidal/homicidal ideation)
- Treatment plan basics
- Medication info
- What's needed for your support

**They won't tell you:**
- Private thoughts/feelings
- Relationship stuff
- Details about substance use beyond medical need
- Therapy session content

**Best practice:** Doctor talks to teen alone, then brings parent in for "safety plan" conversation.
