# Children's psych ER vs. general ER:

**Children's/Pediatric Psychiatric ER is BETTER if:**

✓ Within 30-45 minutes drive  
✓ Has dedicated psychiatric inpatient unit  
✓ Staff trained specifically for teens  
✓ Environment designed for mental health crisis  

**Advantages:**
- Faster psychiatric evaluation (staff on-site)
- Better at de-escalation with teens
- If admission needed, seamless transfer to their psych unit
- More appropriate environment (not next to gunshot wounds/car accidents)

**General ER is FINE if:**
- Children's ER is 1+ hours away
- You need help RIGHT NOW
- It has psychiatric consultation available

**All ERs can:**
- Medically clear him
- Get psychiatric consult (may take longer)
- Admit to psych unit (theirs or transfer)
- Discharge with safety plan and referrals

**Ask pediatrician:** "If you're sending us to ER, which one do you recommend?"

They'll know which has better psychiatric resources.

**Don't drive an extra hour to children's ER if:**
- He's actively dangerous to himself
- He's extremely unstable
- It's late at night (fewer specialized staff anyway)

**Closer is better in true emergency. Specialized is better if you have time.**

"If you won't take what the doctor prescribed, I have to take you back to get evaluated again, maybe at the hospital. I'd rather do this at home with you cooperating. What do you want to do?"

**Most teens cave when they realize:**
- You won't drop it
- They still feel awful
- Hospital is the alternative
