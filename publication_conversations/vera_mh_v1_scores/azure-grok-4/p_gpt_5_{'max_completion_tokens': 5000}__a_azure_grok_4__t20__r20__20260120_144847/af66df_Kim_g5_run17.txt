### Quick Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
This is about tensing and releasing muscle groups to release physical tension, which often quiets the mental hum too. It should take 5-10 minutes. Get comfy (maybe stay seated with Miso in your lap), and we'll go step by step. Breathe deeply throughout—in through nose, out through mouth.

1. **Feet and Legs**: Curl your toes tightly for 5 seconds (tense!), then release and let them relax completely for 10 seconds. Feel the difference? Now, tense your calf muscles (point your toes up if it helps), hold for 5, release for 10. Shake out any leftover tension.

2. **Thighs and Buttocks**: Squeeze your thigh muscles and glutes as if you're gripping a chair—hold for 5, then let go for 10. Notice the warmth or tingling as they relax.

3. **Abdomen and Chest**: Tighten your stomach muscles (like bracing for a punch), hold for 5, release for 10. Then, take a deep breath to expand your chest, tense it, hold, and exhale fully to release.

4. **Hands and Arms**: Make fists and tense your arms (bend at elbows if you want), hold for 5, release for 10. Stretch your fingers out wide afterward.

5. **Shoulders and Neck**: Shrug your shoulders up to your ears, tense for 5, drop and release for 10. Gently roll your neck if it feels good (but skip if it's sore).

6. **Face and Head**: Scrunch your face (frown, squint), hold for 5, release for 10. Finally, tense your jaw (clench lightly), hold, and let go.
