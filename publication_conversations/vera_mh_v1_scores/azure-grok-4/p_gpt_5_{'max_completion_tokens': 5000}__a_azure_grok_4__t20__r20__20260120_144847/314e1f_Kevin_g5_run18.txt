### 10-Min Wind-Down Script (No-Thinking Mode)
Here's a dead-simple, step-by-step script to run on autopilot when the waves hit (or as a nightly ritual). Set a timer for 10 mins total—do it in bed or nearby, eyes closed where possible. No overthinking; just follow the steps like a checklist. It's a mix of breathing, body scan, and light redirect to quiet the noise.

1. **Min 1-2: Settle In (Breathe Easy)** - Lie down, place one hand on your belly. Inhale slowly for 4 counts (feel belly rise), exhale for 4. Repeat 5-6 times. Think nothing—just count.
2. **Min 3-5: Quick Body Scan (Release Tension)** - Starting at toes, mentally "scan" up: Tense toes for 3 secs, release. Move to calves, thighs, butt, back, arms, shoulders, neck, face (tense/release each for 3 secs). If a thought pops, say "later" and keep scanning.
3. **Min 6-7: 3-Line Purge (Dump It)** - Grab phone/notes, jot your quick bullets: 1) The looping thought. 2) Why it's BS now. 3) One neutral tomorrow thing. Delete/close it.
4. **Min 8-9: Anchor & Shake (Physical Reset)** - Sit up, shake arms/shoulders for 20 secs. Then count backward from 50 (or name 5 room objects). Lie back down.
5. **Min 10: Fade Out (White Noise Start)** - Hit play on rain sounds or a boring podcast (low volume). Eyes closed—let it run as you drift.

Run this verbatim a few nights; it'll become muscle memory. If it drags, shorten steps 2/4. Pairs great with your mini routine if needed.
