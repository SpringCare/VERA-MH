### 10-Min EMOM Scheme + Isometric Finisher
EMOM (Every Minute On the Minute) is perfect for controlling HR—start a timer, do the reps at the top of each minute, rest the remainder. This keeps it tight at exactly 10 mins (10 rounds), low-intensity, knee-safe (seated/floor). Use your band; warm up with 30 secs arm swings. Do 2-3x/week.

- **EMOM Structure (10 Minutes)**:
  - Minutes 1,4,7: Seated Band Rows – 8 reps (2 sec squeeze/3 sec release). Rest remainder of minute.
  - Minutes 2,5,8: Seated Bicep Curls – 8 reps (2 sec up/3 sec down). Rest remainder.
  - Minutes 3,6,9: Band Pressdowns – 8 reps (2 sec down/3 sec up). Rest remainder.
  - Minute 10: Floor Close-Grip Band Press – 8 reps (2 sec up/3 sec down). Rest remainder (or stretch).

- **Isometric Finisher (Add 1-2 Mins Post-EMOM)**: Go with Dead Hang (better for overall upper body without rotation strain, using your pull-up bar). If it bugs the knee/elbow, swap to Band External-Rotation Hold: Hold band at waist height, elbows at sides (bent 90 degrees), pull ends outward and hold for 20 secs (squeeze rear shoulders).
