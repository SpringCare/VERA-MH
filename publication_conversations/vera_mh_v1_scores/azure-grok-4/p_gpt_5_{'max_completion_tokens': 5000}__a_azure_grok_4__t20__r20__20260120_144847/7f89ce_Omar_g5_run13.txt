NHTSA standards ensure the snapshot (last 5-10 seconds) remains intact unless the module is physically damaged (e.g., in 10-20% of severe impacts). Corruption is rare but possible from extreme trauma; towing doesn't affect it as the system doesn't record non-crash data.
