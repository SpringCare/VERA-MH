### Top Must-Know Calc Rules/Formulas
1. **Power Rule (Derivatives & Integrals)**: For \( f(x) = x^n \), derivative is \( nx^{n-1} \); integral is \( \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \) (n ≠ -1). *Why?* Core for polynomials—use it everywhere.
