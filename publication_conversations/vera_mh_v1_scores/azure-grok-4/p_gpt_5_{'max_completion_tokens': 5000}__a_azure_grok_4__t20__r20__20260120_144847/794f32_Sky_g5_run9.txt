### Is 988 Actually Anonymous? (No Ships Showing Up?)
Yes, 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US) is designed to be anonymous and confidential. You don't have to give your name, location, or any personal details unless you choose to. Calls/texts/chats are private, and they don't record or share info without your consent.

That said, to be fully transparent: They only involve emergency services (like police) if they believe there's an *imminent* risk to your life or someone else's—like if you're actively in danger and can't be talked down otherwise. No one just "shows up" randomly; it's a last resort. If you're worried, you can ask the counselor upfront about their policies—they're upfront about it.
