### Quick Loop-Breakers for ADHD Brains
1. **Sensory Overload Swap**: Your brain's latching onto the bad image because it's stimulating (in a shitty way). Flip it by blasting something else sensory that's way more intense but neutral/positive. Crank up music with heavy beats or lyrics you can hyper-focus on dissecting (think rap battles or metal—something chaotic). Or chew on something super sour/crunchy (like ice or a Warhead candy) to force your senses to redirect. It's like hitting the reset button on your attention—works fast because ADHD thrives on novelty.

2. **5-4-3-2-1 Grounding**: This is a classic for rumination but ADHD-ified: Name 5 things you see right now (be super detailed, like "the scuff on that wall from when I moved the couch"), 4 you can touch (feel textures around you), 3 you hear (even subtle shit like the fridge hum), 2 you smell, 1 you taste. It pulls your brain out of the abstract loop and into the concrete present. Takes under 2 minutes, no tools needed—do it seated or laying down.

3. **Externalize the Thought**: ADHD brains often loop because the thought's stuck inside. Grab a notebook (or voice-to-text on your phone if writing's a drag) and dump the exact image/word-for-word what's playing in your head. Then, challenge it like a debate: "Okay, what's the evidence this is even happening? What's the counter?" It turns the loop into a task you can "finish," which can satisfy that fixation urge. If it feels too structured, just rant it out loud to yourself like you're arguing with a friend.

4. **Body Hacks Without Moving**: Since the knee's out, try a quick physical interrupt that's low-key: Splash cold water on your face or hold an ice pack to your neck/forehead for 30 seconds—it shocks the nervous system and can snap you out of overdrive. Or do seated "fidget" moves like tapping your fingers in patterns or squeezing a stress ball—keeps the hyperactivity channeled without standing.

5. **Timer Trick**: Set a 5-minute timer and give yourself permission to "do the loop" full-blast—lean into it hard. When the timer dings, switch to something else mandatory (like watching a dumb 5-min YouTube clip). It's counterintuitive, but for ADHD, scheduling the obsession can make it less sticky because your brain knows it's got an end.
