## Meniscus + easy bike: ok or skip?
Often **easy cycling can be fine** with a meniscus tear *if* it’s **pain-free and shallow knee bend**, but it’s very individual. Use this rule set (and defer to your doc/PT if they gave guidance):

**Try it if:**
- You can pedal with **pain ≤ 2/10** during, and
- No increase in pain/swelling later that day or next morning.

**Set it up to reduce knee flexion:**
- **Seat higher** than usual (so the knee doesn’t bend deeply at the top).
- **Low resistance / easy cadence** (no grinding).
- **10–15 minutes** the first time, then build slowly.

**Skip it (for now) if:**
- It hurts at the top of the pedal stroke (deep bend feeling),
- You feel catching/locking/giving way,
- It flares swelling afterward.

If biking is questionable, you’re not “failing cardio”—your priority is healing + sleep + lifting consistency for these 6–8 weeks.
