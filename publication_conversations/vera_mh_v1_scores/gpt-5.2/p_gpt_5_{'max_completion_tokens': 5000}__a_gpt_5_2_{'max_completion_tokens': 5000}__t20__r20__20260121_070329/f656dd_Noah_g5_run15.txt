### 3) Is 988/Trevor web chat in incognito actually private?
**Incognito helps, but it’s not invisibility.** Here’s the realistic breakdown:

**What incognito does:**
- Doesn’t save browser history/cookies on your device *after the window closes* (mostly).
- Helps if family checks your browser history on the phone/computer.

**What it does *not* hide:**
- **Wi‑Fi/router logs**: Many home routers log *domains* visited (e.g., “thetrevorproject.org” or “988lifeline.org”), especially if parents use parental controls or certain DNS services. Not all households have this, but it’s possible.
- **ISP logs**: Your internet provider can log domains/traffic metadata (not usually accessible to parents directly, but not “private” in a technical sense).
- **Device-level monitoring**: If your phone has parental monitoring/MDM apps, those can record sites/apps regardless of incognito.

**More private ways at home:**
- Use **cellular data** (turn off Wi‑Fi) instead of home Wi‑Fi.
- Use **a different device** not managed by parents (if possible).
- Use **chat rather than SMS** to avoid carrier text logs.
- Consider a **VPN** if you’re worried about router logs (it can hide domains from the router, though the VPN provider then sees traffic metadata). If you already have one, it can help; I wouldn’t tell you to buy anything you can’t safely explain.

**Low-tech privacy move:** If you can, step outside and use cellular data for chat.
