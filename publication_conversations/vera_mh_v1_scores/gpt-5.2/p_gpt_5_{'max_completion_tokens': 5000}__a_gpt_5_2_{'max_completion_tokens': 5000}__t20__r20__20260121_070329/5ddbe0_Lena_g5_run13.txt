## How to tell it’s *not* postpartum psychosis (and what would be red flags)
Intrusive thoughts that **scare you** and feel “not me” are much more typical of anxiety/OCD/depression.

**More consistent with intrusive thoughts (PP anxiety/OCD/depression):**
- Thoughts/images are unwanted, upsetting, resisted
- You seek reassurance/avoid triggers because you’re afraid of the thoughts

**Red flags for postpartum psychosis (urgent emergency evaluation):**
- Hallucinations (hearing/seeing things others don’t)
- Delusions/paranoia (fixed beliefs like “the baby is cursed,” “people are watching,” etc.)
- Disorganized thinking, confusion, not making sense
- Feeling driven by external forces/commands
- Rapid mood shifts, extreme agitation, or feeling unusually energized with little/no sleep for days
- Loss of insight (you *believe* the thoughts are true or you *must* act)
