## Super low-key tools for “what’s the point” waves (no phone required)
These are meant to be invisible if someone barges in.

### 1) The 90-second wave ride (quiet)
- Name it silently: **“This is a wave.”**
- Exhale slowly for a count of 6–8, inhale 4 (no obvious breath-holding).
- Tell yourself: **“I don’t have to solve my whole life tonight. Just get through the next 10 minutes.”**
Set a mental timer: *10 minutes*. Waves usually peak and drop.

### 2) Grounding you can do while sitting there
Pick **5–4–3–2–1** in your head:
- 5 things you see
- 4 things you feel (fabric, feet on floor)
- 3 sounds
- 2 smells
- 1 taste or one thing you’re grateful for (even tiny: “my bed,” “music,” “college break ends”)

### 3) “If-then” micro plan (reduces panic)
Write it on paper if safe, or memorize:
- **If** the wave hits hard, **then** I go to the bathroom, run cold water on wrists for 20 seconds, and do 10 slow exhales.
- **If** it’s still >7/10 after that, **then** I open Trevor chat or text 988 from the bathroom and delete the thread after.
- **If** I start feeling like I might act on it, **then** I call 988 or go to a safe adult immediately.

### 4) A single sentence that helps
A lot of people find this works because it separates *you* from the shame:
> “I’m not unsafe because I’m into guys. I’m distressed because I’m under pressure and scared.”
