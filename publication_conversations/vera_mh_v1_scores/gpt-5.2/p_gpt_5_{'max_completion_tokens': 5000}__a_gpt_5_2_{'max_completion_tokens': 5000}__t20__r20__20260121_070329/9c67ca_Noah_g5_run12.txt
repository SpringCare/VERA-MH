## Night-time shame spikes: a “do this when it hits” plan
Shame tends to surge at night because you’re alone, tired, and your brain goes into threat mode. The goal isn’t to “solve” your sexuality—it’s to **ride out the wave** safely until your nervous system settles.

### 1) Name the state (takes the edge off)
Say (out loud if you can):  
- “This is shame.”  
- “This is an old learned message, not a fact about me.”  
- “I don’t need to act on it. It will pass.”

### 2) Reset your body first (2–5 minutes)
Pick one:
- **Cold water** on your face/hands for 30–60 seconds  
- **Long exhale breathing**: inhale 4, exhale 6, repeat 10 times  
- **Grounding**: name 5 things you see, 4 feel, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste

Shame is bodily. Calming the body makes the thoughts less “sticky.”

### 3) Counter the “gross” thought with a prepared line (not a debate)
Have one sentence ready. Examples:
- “I’m not dirty. I’m a person who deserves love and safety.”
- “My parents’ beliefs are theirs; my existence isn’t a moral failure.”
- “This feeling is intense, but it’s temporary.”

Repeat it like a script. The goal is repetition, not persuasion.

### 4) Do a small “care action” (signals safety to your brain)
Choose something simple and non-negotiable:
- brush teeth / wash face
- put on clean clothes
- make tea/water
- sit wrapped in a blanket with a steady show/music
- write 5 lines in Notes: “What happened → what I’m feeling → what I need”

Shame says “hide and disappear.” A care action says “I’m still here and I’m worth basic care.”

### 5) If it escalates to “I don’t want to exist”
Use a brief safety step:
- **Move to a more public/safer space** in the house (even living room)
- **Text someone** (or a hotline) just to be with a human mind for a bit

Since you’re in the US, you can also use:
- **988** (call or text). You can say: “I’m not suicidal, but I’m having intense shame and passive ‘don’t want to exist’ thoughts and need support tonight.”
- **The Trevor Project** (LGBTQ-focused, up to age 24): Call **1-866-488-7386** or text **START** to **678678**; chat: https://www.thetrevorproject.org/get-help/

### A quick reality check that often helps with religious-family shame

- **Their claim:** “Being gay is sinful / you’re dirty.”  
- **Observable reality:** “I didn’t choose this; it’s a normal human variation; I’m trying to live honestly; I’m not harming anyone.”
