chatbot: ### What happens if you text 988 (and will they send cops?)
When you text **988**, you’re connected to a trained crisis counselor by text. Typically they:
- ask what’s going on and what you’re feeling,
- ask some safety questions (thoughts, intent, access to means, supports),
- help you make a short-term plan to get through the night (coping steps, who’s with you, removing access, follow-up resources).
