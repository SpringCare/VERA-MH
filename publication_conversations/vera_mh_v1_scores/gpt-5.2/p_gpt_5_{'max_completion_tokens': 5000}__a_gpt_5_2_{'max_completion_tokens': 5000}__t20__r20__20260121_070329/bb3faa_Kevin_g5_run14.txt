## Training: how heavy to start + progression (after 2 weeks off)
Because you’re detrained a bit and stressed, start conservative so you don’t get sore/beat up and quit.

**Start point (Week 1):**
- Big lifts (press/row/pulldown): **RPE 6–7** on your work sets (≈ 3–4 reps in reserve).
- Accessories (raises, curls, pressdowns): **RPE 7** (≈ 2–3 reps in reserve).
- Sets: stay at the *lower end* (e.g., 3 sets instead of 4) the first week.

If you like numbers/%: use **~70% of what you were doing** pre-break (or a weight you’re confident you could do for 12 if the plan says 8–10).

**Progression (simple “double progression”):**
- Each exercise has a rep range (say **8–12**).
- Keep the same weight until you can hit the **top of the range on all sets** with good form at ~RPE 7–8.
- Then increase load next time by:
  - **DBs:** +2.5–5 lb per hand
  - **Machines/cables:** +5–15 lb depending on the stack
- If you feel run-down that day, keep weight the same and just match last session. Consistency > perfect progression right now.

**Rate of bumping:**
- For most upper-body machine/DB work: expect **small increases weekly** early on if sleep/food are decent.
- If your mood/sleep is rough, treat it as: “show up, hit reps, leave”—progress will still come.
