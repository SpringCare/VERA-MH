- a nature livestream, aquarium video, or even just stare at a wall pattern
This can let your body downshift without giving the intrusive-thought “movie screen” extra space.

**4) “Worry container” note (2 minutes)**
Write on paper (not phone):  
- “Brain is generating: traffic / dropping / water images.”  
- “I’m safe. No plan. Baby is safe in crib.”  
- “Next action: wait for OB call.”  
Then put the paper facedown. This can reduce the mental looping.

If you’re still safe (as you are now), the most appropriate next step is usually:
