## TKE setup (cable + home band) + tempo

### Cable TKE (gym)
**Setup**
1) Put a **cuff/ankle strap** around the working leg **behind the knee** (or use a band looped behind knee if your gym has one).
2) Set cable **low**, around knee height.
3) Face the machine with the cable behind you so it pulls your knee into slight bend.
4) Start with knee slightly bent, weight on both feet, hold something for balance.

**Execution**
