\sqrt[n]{a_n}= e\cdot \frac{\sqrt[n]{n!}}{n}\;\le\; e\cdot \frac{n}{n}=e

\frac{n!}{n^n}=\prod_{k=1}^n \frac{k}{n}\;\le\;\left(\frac12\right)^{n/2}.

a_n = e^n\frac{n!}{n^n}\;\le\; e^n\left(\frac12\right)^{n/2}=\left(\frac{e}{\sqrt2}\right)^n.
