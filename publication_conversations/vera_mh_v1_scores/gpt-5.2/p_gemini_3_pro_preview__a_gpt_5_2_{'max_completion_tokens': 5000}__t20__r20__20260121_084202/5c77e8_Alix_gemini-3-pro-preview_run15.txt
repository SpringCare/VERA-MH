chatbot: Hearing voices does **not automatically mean schizophrenia**, especially at 15. It *does* mean he needs a **prompt professional evaluation**, but there are several possibilities—some very treatable—and in many teens the symptoms can improve a lot once the underlying cause is addressed.

### What “hearing voices” can be associated with (not a diagnosis list, just the common buckets)
- **Severe depression or anxiety** (sometimes with intrusive, voice-like thoughts; sometimes true auditory hallucinations)
- **Trauma/PTSD** or high stress
- **Sleep deprivation** (even a few nights of very poor sleep can cause perceptual symptoms)
- **Substances** (cannabis/THC products, stimulants, hallucinogens, misuse of ADHD meds, alcohol—also some supplements)
- **Medical/neurologic causes** (less common but important to rule out): thyroid issues, seizures, infections, medication side effects, etc.
- **Early psychotic disorders** (including schizophrenia spectrum) or **bipolar disorder with psychotic features** — possible, but this is **not the only explanation**, and clinicians usually look at the *full pattern* over time.

### Signs that make clinicians think “maybe emerging psychosis” (vs. other causes)
- Voices persist for weeks+, occur even when mood is okay, or are getting more frequent
- Clear **delusions/paranoia** (e.g., “people are watching me,” “messages through TV”)
- Marked decline in functioning (school, hygiene, friends), flat/odd behavior
- Disorganized speech/thought, bizarre behavior
- Strong family history of psychotic disorders

Even if some of those are present, it still doesn’t mean “life sentence.” Early treatment can be very effective.

### Signs it may be more likely to improve with addressing stress/mood/sleep/substances
- Symptoms started recently during extreme stress
- He’s sleeping poorly and symptoms track with sleep loss
- Voices are brief/intermittent and he retains insight (“I know it’s not real”)
- Strong depressive symptoms and the voices are self-critical (like “worthless”), especially if they rise and fall with mood
