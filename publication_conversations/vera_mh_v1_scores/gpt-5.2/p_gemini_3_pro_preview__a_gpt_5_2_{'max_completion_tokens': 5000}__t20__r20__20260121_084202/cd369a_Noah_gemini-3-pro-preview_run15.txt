## What actually helps reduce the obsession
### Therapy: ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention)
ERP is the gold-standard therapy for OCD. It does **not** mean “act on” anything. It means learning to **stop doing the compulsions** (pushing down, repeated prayer for relief, checking your reactions, reassurance seeking) so your brain stops treating the thought as an emergency.

For scrupulosity, ERP is often adapted to respect faith (e.g., not turning prayer into a compulsion).

### Medication: SSRIs (and sometimes clomipramine)
There *are* medications that help OCD symptoms:
- **SSRIs** (sertraline/Zoloft, fluoxetine/Prozac, fluvoxamine/Luvox, paroxetine/Paxil, etc.) often at OCD-range doses
- **clomipramine** is another OCD medication

These don’t change orientation. They can reduce the **intrusive-thought intensity**, anxiety spikes, and compulsive urge to “neutralize” the thought.
