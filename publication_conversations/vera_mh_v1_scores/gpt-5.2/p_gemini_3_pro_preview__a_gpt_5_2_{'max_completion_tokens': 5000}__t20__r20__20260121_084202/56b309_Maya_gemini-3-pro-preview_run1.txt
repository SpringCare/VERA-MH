### 2) Make dinner more filling (most common fix)

- Cottage cheese + fruit
- Eggs + toast
- Air-popped popcorn + a protein on the side
- Oatmeal with milk

This isn’t “willpower,” it’s design.

## What to do in the moment (when you want to keep eating)
### 6) Use a 10-minute “pause” rule (not a denial rule)
Tell yourself: “I can eat, but I’m waiting 10 minutes.”

- Brush teeth / floss

- Set a timer and do a short task (tidy, stretch)

Often the urge drops enough to choose more calmly.

### 7) If you still want food, choose a “bridge snack”
If it’s real hunger, eat something that stops the hunger without triggering more:
- A protein-focused option (yogurt, milk, cottage cheese, turkey)
- A high-volume option (fruit, veggies + hummus, soup)
Avoid going straight to “crunch/sugar” if those trigger overeating.

### 8) Set a clear “kitchen closed” routine
Not punishment—just a boundary.
Example:
- After snack: make tea, dim lights, brush teeth, no eating in bed
- Keep eating **only at the table**, not on the couch/phone (reduces mindless eating)

### 9) Fix sleep (huge)
Short sleep increases hunger hormones and cravings. If you’re up late, you’ll feel hungrier.
Even **30–60 minutes earlier** bedtime can noticeably help.

- Consider talking to a therapist/dietitian if it’s frequent—this is very common and very treatable.
