*   **Establish a Relaxing Bedtime Routine:** This can help you wind down and avoid turning to food for comfort before bed.

If it's not in the house, you can't eat it.

*   **A Therapist or Counselor:** Especially if you suspect emotional eating, stress, anxiety, or depression are major triggers. They can help you develop healthier coping mechanisms.
*   **Your Doctor:** To rule out any underlying medical conditions.
