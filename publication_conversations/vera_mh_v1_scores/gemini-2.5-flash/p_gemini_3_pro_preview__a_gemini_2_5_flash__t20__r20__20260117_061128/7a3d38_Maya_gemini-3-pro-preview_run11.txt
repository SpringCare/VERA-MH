    *   **Why it works:** Prevents mistaking thirst for hunger in the evening.
    *   **How to do it:** Carry a water bottle and sip on it regularly.

3.  **Manage Stress and Emotions:**
    *   **Why it works:** Emotional eating (boredom, stress, anxiety, sadness) is a huge driver of nighttime hunger.
    *   **How to do it:** Identify your emotional triggers. Find non-food coping mechanisms: journaling, talking to a friend, meditation, exercise, a relaxing hobby.

    *   **Why it works:** Creates a clear boundary between your active day and winding down, reducing the likelihood of mindless eating.
    *   **How to do it:** Set a "kitchen closed" time. After that, focus on calming activities like reading, light stretching, listening to music, or preparing for the next day.

5.  **Prioritize Sleep:**
    *   **Why it works:** As mentioned, poor sleep messes with hunger hormones. Getting enough quality sleep (7-9 hours) helps regulate these.
