### For the ADHD Brain That Won't Shut Up at Night:

1.  **The "Brain Dump" Journal (Pre-Sleep Strategy):**
    *   **How it works:** About 1-2 hours before you plan to sleep, or whenever your thoughts start to race, grab a notebook and a pen. Write down *everything* that's on your mind. 2.  **Structured "Worry Time" (Earlier in the Day):**
    *   **How it works:** Designate 15-20 minutes *earlier in the day* (e.g., mid-afternoon, but definitely not close to bedtime) as your "worry time." 3.  **Sensory Grounding (When Thoughts Are Overwhelming):**
    *   **How it works:** When your mind is racing and you feel overwhelmed, try the "5-4-3-2-1" technique:
        *   Notice **5** things you can see.
        *   Notice **4** things you can feel (e.g., the texture of your clothes, the chair under you, the air on your skin).
        *   Notice **3** things you can hear.
        *   Notice **2** things you can smell.
        *   Notice **1** thing you can taste (or something in your mouth, like your tongue).
    *   **Why it helps:** This pulls your mind out of abstract thoughts and into the present moment, engaging your senses. It can break the cycle of overthinking.

4.  **Background Noise/Story (Non-Stimulating):**
    *   **How it works:** Instead of silence, which can be a breeding ground for racing thoughts, try a "boring" podcast, an audiobook you've heard before, or even white noise/nature sounds. The key is *non-stimulating*. You want something that gives your ADHD brain *just enough* to latch onto so it doesn't create its *own* overwhelming stimulation.
