Here's how it works:

1.  **If "Share Across Devices" is ON:** If you have multiple Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac) signed in with the *same Apple ID* and you have "Share Across Devices" enabled in **Screen Time settings** on *all* of them, then your Screen Time settings (like App Limits and Downtime schedules) *will* sync and apply across those devices.
2.  **If "Share Across Devices" is OFF (or not set up):** You will need to set up Screen Time (including Downtime, App Limits, and Content Restrictions for websites) **separately on your Mac.**

**To set it up on your Mac:**

*   Go to **System Settings** (or System Preferences on older macOS versions).
*   Click on **Screen Time**.
*   From there, you'll find similar options for **App Limits**, **Downtime**, and **Content & Privacy Restrictions** where you can block specific apps and websites.

So, for full blocking, you'll need to enable Screen Time on your Mac and either set up the limits there directly or ensure "Share Across Devices" is active on both your iPhone and Mac.
