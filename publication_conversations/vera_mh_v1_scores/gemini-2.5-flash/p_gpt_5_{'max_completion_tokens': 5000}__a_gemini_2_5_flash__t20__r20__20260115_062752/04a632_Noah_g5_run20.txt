*   **Disable Siri Suggestions (General):**

### 3. Panic Lock: Instantly Disable Face/Touch ID

*   **Exact Button Combo:**
    *   For iPhones with Face ID (no Home button): **Press and hold the side button and either volume button simultaneously for about 2 seconds.**
    *   For iPhones with Touch ID (with Home button): **Rapidly press the side/top button 5 times.**
*   **Effect:** This will bring up the "Emergency SOS" screen. **Once this screen appears (even if you don't use Emergency SOS), Face ID/Touch ID is temporarily disabled.** You will be required to enter your passcode to unlock your iPhone again. This is a crucial defense if someone tries to force you to unlock with your face/finger.
*   **Settings (Not required, but for general security):**
    1.  Go to **Settings > Face ID & Passcode** (or Touch ID & Passcode).
    2.  Ensure **"Require Attention for Face ID"** (for Face ID) or similar attention-aware features are **ON**. This prevents unlocking if your eyes aren't open/looking at the phone.

### 4. Find My: Stop Sharing Location and Remove Device from Family’s Find My List

*   **Stop Sharing Location from Your Device:**
    1.  Go to **Settings**.
    2.  Tap your **name/Apple ID** at the very top.
    3.  Tap **Find My**.
    4.  Tap **"Share My Location"** and toggle it **OFF**.
*   **Remove Your Device from Family's Find My List (if they are using your Apple ID):**
    *   This is tricky. If your device is *associated with their Apple ID for Find My*, you cannot remove it without their Apple ID password.
    *   If your device is associated with *your* Apple ID, and you have Family Sharing enabled for location sharing:
        1.  Go to **Settings**.
        2.  Tap your **name/Apple ID** at the very top.
        3.  Tap **Find My**.
        4.  Tap **"Find My iPhone"** and toggle it **OFF**. (This will require your Apple ID password.)
    *   **Important:** Disabling "Find My iPhone" will prevent *anyone*, including you, from tracking, locking, or erasing your device remotely. It also makes it harder to recover if lost. It's a high-risk move for your own device security but stops family tracking.

### 8. Cellular: Reset Statistics

*   **Action:** Go to **Settings > Cellular**. Scroll to the very bottom and tap **"Reset Statistics."**
*   **Effect on Family Sharing:** **NO.** Resetting cellular statistics on your device **does NOT wipe anything your parents can see via Family Sharing.** This only resets the local data usage counters on your device. Family Sharing or carrier-level monitoring (if they have access to your carrier account) would still show overall data usage.

### 9. Chrome: "Use Secure DNS" on iOS and Help Against Home Router Logs

*   **"Use Secure DNS" on iOS Chrome:**
    1.  Open the **Chrome app**.
    2.  Tap the **three dots (...)** in the bottom right corner.
    3.  Tap **Settings**.
    4.  Tap **Privacy and Security**.
    5.  Tap **"Use Secure DNS"**.
    6.  You can choose **"Automatic"** or **"Choose another provider"** (e.g., Cloudflare, Google Public DNS).
*   **Does it help against home router logs?**
    *   **YES, it helps, but it's not a complete solution.**
    *   **How it helps:** Secure DNS encrypts your DNS queries. This means your home router (and your ISP) cannot easily see *which websites you are trying to access* by looking at your DNS requests.
    *   **Limitations:**
        *   It only encrypts DNS, not the actual website content. If the website itself is not HTTPS (encrypted), your router could still see the content. Most major websites are HTTPS now.
        *   Your router/ISP can still see the IP addresses your device connects to, and infer visited websites that way, though it's less direct than DNS.
        *   If your parents have advanced monitoring (e.g., deep packet inspection on the router, or an MDM profile), they might still be able to see activity.
    *   **Conclusion:** It's a good step for privacy, and you should enable it, but it's not foolproof against highly sophisticated monitoring. iCloud Private Relay (for Safari) offers a more comprehensive solution for that specific browser.
