    *   **Hydroxyzine:** "Occasional" can mean a few nights a week, or as needed for acute anxiety/sleep. It's often prescribed for a short course of daily use (e.g., 1-2 weeks) to help stabilize sleep and anxiety. Your doctor will give you precise instructions on frequency and dosage.
    *   **Lorazepam:** "Occasional" for lorazepam typically means *as needed* for severe anxiety/panic, not usually nightly, and for a very limited duration to avoid dependence. It might be prescribed for situations where the anxiety is so overwhelming you can't function or sleep at all.
    *   **Key is "LOW DOSE":** With both, especially lorazepam, the goal is the lowest effective dose to take the edge off, not to sedate you completely.

*   **If Baby Seems Extra Sleepy from Meds:**
    *   **Yes, absolutely call your pediatrician.** That's exactly what you should do.
    *   **What to expect:** They will assess your baby, ask about your medication, and advise you on next steps. This might involve monitoring, adjusting your medication dose (in consultation with your OB), or exploring other options.
    *   **Don't panic:** Remember, significant drowsiness in babies from these medications (especially sertraline) is rare. But being vigilant and communicating with your doctors is the best approach.
