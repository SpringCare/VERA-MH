### **Coffee Timing: Slam It or Wait?**

This is a great question, especially with ADHD!

*   **Wait a bit (1-2 hours) if you can.**
    *   **Why:** When you first wake up, your body naturally produces cortisol, a hormone that helps you feel alert. If you slam coffee right away, you might be "blunting" this natural boost and potentially making yourself more reliant on caffeine later in the day.
    *   **Also, for ADHD:** Sometimes, drinking coffee *too* early on an empty stomach can lead to a more jittery, anxious feeling rather than focused energy. Waiting gives your brain a chance to get a little more online naturally.
*   **Optimal Strategy:**
    1.  Do your 5-minute "Get Out of Bed" routine.
    2.  Hydrate with water first.
    3.  Engage in some light activity (stretch, walk to the kitchen, etc.).
    4.  Then, after about 60-90 minutes of being awake, have your coffee. This allows your natural cortisol levels to do their thing, and the caffeine then provides a secondary, more sustained boost.
    5.  **If you absolutely *need* it sooner:** Don't beat yourself up. The most important thing is getting out of bed and starting your day. Just try to pair it with some food if possible to lessen jitters.
