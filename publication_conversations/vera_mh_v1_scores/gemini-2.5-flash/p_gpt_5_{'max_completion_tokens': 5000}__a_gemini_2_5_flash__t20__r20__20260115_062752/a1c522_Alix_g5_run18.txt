### **Re-confirming TX Emergency Detention (ED) Timing – Exact:**

*   Okay, let's get this as precise as possible for Texas:
    1.  **Initial Emergency Detention (ED) Order:** This is the *initial legal authorization* to take someone into custody for mental health evaluation.     2.  **Physician's Emergency Detention (PED) Order:** Once at a facility (like an ER or psychiatric hospital), a physician (or qualified mental health professional) assesses the person.     3.  **Total Time:** So, in practical terms, you're looking at an initial 24 hours for transport/first assessment, followed by up to 48 *business hours* for a more thorough evaluation and stabilization.     4.  **After PED:** If, after the PED period, he still needs inpatient care and is unwilling to stay voluntarily, the facility must then file for a **Temporary Order for Protective Custody (TOPC)** through the courts, which can extend the hold for a longer period (e.g., up to 14-30 days) for further treatment, requiring a court hearing.
