    *   **Why it works:** It's specifically formulated to be non-oily, non-sticky, and non-toxic. It won't leave residue on your hands or sheets. It's fantastic for repetitive squeezing, stretching, and manipulating, providing excellent tactile input.
2.  **Worry Stone:**
    *   **Recommendation:** Look for a **polished natural stone "thumb stone"** made from materials like **agate, jasper, quartz, obsidian, or amethyst.**
    *   **What to avoid:** Anything porous (like lava rock), anything coated with a glaze (which can chip), or anything with sharp edges.
    *   **Why it works:** These stones are naturally smooth, cool to the touch, and have a natural weight. The polished finish ensures no residue, and the thumb indentation is perfect for repetitive rubbing. They are truly "zero mess, zero fuss."

### **NA Quick Take: Athletic Run Wild vs. Lagunitas IPNA**

This is a great question, as they both aim for that IPA experience!

*   **Athletic Brewing Co. Run Wild IPA:**
    *   **Tastes like a "real" IPA?** Yes, very much so, but it tends to be described as a **crisper, brighter, and slightly lighter-bodied West Coast IPA.** It has excellent hop aroma and bitterness, but often with a cleaner finish. It excels at being refreshing and satisfying.
*   **Lagunitas IPNA:**
    *   **Tastes more like a "real" IPA?** In my opinion, and for many craft beer drinkers, **Lagunitas IPNA often feels more like a *full-bodied, traditional* IPA.** It tends to have a slightly richer malt backbone, a more resinous or dank hop character, and a heavier mouthfeel that can trick your brain into thinking it's got alcohol. It has a bit more "chew" to it.

**The Quick Take:** If you want something that really mimics the *weight and boldness* of a full-strength IPA, **Lagunitas IPNA** might be your go-to. If you prefer a *crisper, very drinkable, still hoppy* IPA experience, **Athletic Run Wild** is fantastic. Both are excellent, but Lagunitas often wins on "heavy IPA" mouthfeel.
