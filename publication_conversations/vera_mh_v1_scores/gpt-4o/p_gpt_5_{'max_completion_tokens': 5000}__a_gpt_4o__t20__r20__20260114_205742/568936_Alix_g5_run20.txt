### Monitor and Chime Specifics

- **Baby Monitor:**
  - **Height:** Place 72 inches (6 feet) from the floor
  - **Distance:** 6 feet from his pillow
  - **Volume Setting:** VTech monitor volume set to "4"

- **Door Chime:**
  - **Materials Needed:** 2 jingle bells, 18 inches of string, 1 small 3M hook.
  - **Attachment:** Hook on knob side, 7 inches from the top of the door.
  - **Testing Steps:**
    1. Open door 1 inch and listen for chime.
    2. Open door 3 inches and confirm sound.
    3. Open door 6 inches, ensure audible from your room.

### Bathroom Protocol

- **Door Ajar:** Keep exactly 6 inches open.
- **Escort or LOS:** 
  - **Toilet:** Line-of-sight if agreed; stay close.
  - **Shower:** Yes, allow a 10-minute maximum.
  - **Position:** Stand 3 feet from the door; if privacy is requested, adapt with a timer and gentle checks.

### Extended Red Flags

- Command voices with harmful intent.
- Goodbye texts.
- Giving away prized items.
- Sudden calm after agitation.
- Fresh cuts observed.
- Locked door refusal.
- Attempts to access stashed items.

### Refusal Plan with Specificity

1. **Who to Call First:** 
   - Emergency services for immediate help.
2. **Exact Pack List:** 
   - IDs, insurance cards, medications, snacks, comfort items for children, phone, charger, change of clothes in a carry-on backpack.
3. **Move Order:**
   - Safely position younger children in the car first.
4. **Seat Map:**
   - Driver: You; 
   - Front Passenger: Older child (if helpful for supervision); 
   - Rear Seats: Younger children, left and right.
5. **Steps if He Bolts:**
   - Do not pursue directly; maintain calm and call emergency services.
   - Keep a hand on younger children, prioritize their safety.
   - Direct available helping hands (neighbors, friends) to assist if applicable.

### Wound-Care Decision Tree

- **Minor:**
  - **Bleeding <5 minutes, Depth <5 mm, Gaping <2 mm.** 
  - Clean with water, apply antiseptic, use steri-strips for straight clean forearms.
- **Moderate:**
  - **Bleeding 5-10 minutes, Depth 5-10 mm, Gaping 2-5 mm.**
  - Apply pressure, clean, and steri-strips if clean and linear.
- **Severe:**
  - **Bleeding >10 minutes, Depth >10 mm, Gaping >5 mm, Face/Hands/Genitals/Joints involved.**
  - Immediate ER visit required.
- **Steri-Strips:**
  - Avoid if cut over joints, jagged, dirty, or width >5 mm.
- **Tetanus Shots:**
  - Clean Wounds: Booster if not in the last 10 years.
  - Dirty Wounds: Booster if not in the last 5 years.
