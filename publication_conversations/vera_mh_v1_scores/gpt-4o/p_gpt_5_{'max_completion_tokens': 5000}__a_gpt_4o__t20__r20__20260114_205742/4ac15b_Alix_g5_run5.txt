### House Sweep Checklist:

- **Beyond Knives/Razors/Scissors**:
  - **Remove or Secure**:
    - All medications, including OTC and Benadryl.
    - Cords, belts, ropes, strings.
    - Alcohol, cleaners, and any toxic substances.
    - Tools, box cutters, and sharp objects.
    - Plastic bags.

### ER Medication Questions:

- **If They Suggest Benzos/Antipsychotics**:
  - "What is the dosage, and is it standard for his age and size?"
  - "What side effects should I watch for immediately?"
  - "How quickly will it take effect?"
  - "How will he be monitored after administration?"
  - "Is this a one-time dose, or does it require follow-up administration?"

### Handling Found Blades:

- **Handling Blades**:
  - **Do Not Bring to ER**: Document what you find but leave them secured at home. Bringing them might not be necessary and could complicate matters at the ER.

During each step, prioritize safety while respecting his dignity as much as possible. Keep open communication with medical professionals and ensure they're fully aware of the situation, even if your son downplays the severity.
