### Privacy and EOBs:

- **Do EOBs Show ICD/CPT?** 
  - Yes, they typically display ICD (diagnosis) and CPT (procedure) codes to subscribers.

- **Who Sees This at Work?**
  - Generally, only the benefits coordinator/HR personnel who handle insurance matters will have access to this information. They adhere to confidentiality practices.

- **Checking Self-Funded Status**:
  - Look for your benefits handbook or access the insurance company's portal to find details without contacting HR. 

- **Paying Cash/Telehealth**: 
  - Paying out-of-pocket for telehealth keeps the transaction off company systems.

- **HSA/FSA Reimbursement**:
  - These typically require itemized receipts, which would include provider details. While you can choose generic receipts, ensure your provider can arrange for this. Paperless options depend on your HSA/FSA providers.
