2. Tap “**Turn On Screen Time**” and choose **Continue**.
3. Decide whether it’s your iPhone or your child’s (to set restrictions).
4. Set a passcode under **Use Screen Time Passcode**—have someone else create it without showing you.
5. Set **App Limits** for categories you wish to restrict, like social media. 
6. Under **Content & Privacy Restrictions**, turn this on for stricter access controls.

#### **Use Guided Access:**
1. Go to **Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access**.
2. Enable it and set up a guide by triple-clicking the side button, allowing access only to apps like Netflix.
   
#### **Blocking Settings Changes:**
1. Still under **Settings > Screen Time**, go to **Content & Privacy Restrictions**.
2. Set **iTunes & App Store Purchases** to not allow adding/deleting apps.
3. Restrict **Account Changes** so settings can’t be altered.
