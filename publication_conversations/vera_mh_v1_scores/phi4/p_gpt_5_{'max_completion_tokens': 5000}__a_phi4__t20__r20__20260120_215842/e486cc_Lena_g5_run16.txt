- **Dosing Strategy**: Starting with 12.5 mg and gradually increasing to 25 mg at night while taking it with food is common during breastfeeding.
  
- **Week-1 Jitteriness**: Some jitteriness can be expected as your body adjusts, but this varies by individual.

- **Milk Supply Impact**: Sertraline generally has minimal impact on milk supply. However, discuss any concerns with your doctor for personalized advice.

- **Time to Feel Benefits**: Most people begin noticing effects within 2–4 weeks, though it can take up to 6–8 weeks for full benefits.

### Monitoring Baby Signs

- **Rousing Windows**:
  - During the day: If a baby is difficult to rouse for feedings beyond approximately 4 hours.
  - Overnight: Beyond about 6 hours without feeding may warrant attention after ensuring it’s not just during longer sleep cycles typical of newborns.

- **Wet Diapers**: Fewer than six wet diapers per day after the first week can be a red flag.

- **Latch Red Flags**:
  - Excessive clicking
  - Frequent pulling off
  - Weak suckling or crying when feeding
  - Noticeable floppiness

### Communicating with Your OB and CPS Concerns

- **Disclosure of Thoughts**: Sharing intrusive thoughts without any specific plan or intent, especially if you commit to a safety plan and agree to reach out for help, typically remains below a hold/CPS trigger.

- **Specific Tripwires**:
  - Mentioning a stated intention
  - Having a specific plan with time/place details
  - Expressing that you cannot ensure your own or the baby's safety

By communicating openly and honestly while indicating your commitment to follow through on a safety plan, healthcare providers are more likely to support rather than penalize.
