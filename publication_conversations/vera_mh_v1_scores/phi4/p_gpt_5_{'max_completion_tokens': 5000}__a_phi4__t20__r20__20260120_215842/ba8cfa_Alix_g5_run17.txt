- **Response Time**: The typical ETA can range from 30 minutes to an hour, depending on current demand and staffing. However, for the most accurate information, it's best to speak directly with them upon calling.

- **Emergency Detention**:
  - Under Texas Health & Safety Code (HSC) §573, MCOT clinicians can initiate emergency detention. The written authorization is typically done by a Licensed Mental Health Professional (LMHP). If further legal authority is required beyond the initial intervention, involvement from law enforcement or a magistrate may be necessary.

### Dell Children’s Medical Center:

- **ED Entrance and Location**:
  - Use the main entrance labeled "Emergency Department" at 4900 Mueller Blvd. 

- **Direct ED Contact**: 
  - Dial (512) 324‑8500 for guidance upon arrival.

- **Parking Details**:
  - Follow signs to designated Patient/Visitor Parking Lots near the entrance on Mueller Blvd. Specific lot names like P1 or P2 aren't typically specified publicly, so signage will direct you appropriately once there.

- **Admissions and Transfers**:
  - Dell Children’s generally admits teens for psychiatric care; however, transfers may occur if specialized treatment is needed, possibly to facilities such as Shoal Creek Hospital.

- **Psych Evaluation Wait Times**:
  - Urgent cases are prioritized. While exact wait times can vary overnight, it tends to be quicker during these hours due to reduced demand.

### Handling Refusal of Procedures:

- **Parental Consent**: Your consent can aid in compliance for procedures like urine tests and labs.
  
- **Restraints/Sedation**:
  - These measures are considered only if there is a critical safety concern for the patient or staff.

### Packing Essentials:

- **What to Pack**:
  - Charger, hoodie (consider without strings), shoes with Velcro straps, comfort item (soft toy/book), and snacks/drinks. Confirm snack policy directly upon arrival.
  
- **Items Potentially Taken Away**: Items like shoelaces and string hoods may be removed for safety reasons.

### EMS Transport:

- **Transporting Without You**:
  - EMS can take him without you if phone consent is provided. Ensure all necessary information is shared with the EMS team for registration.

### Leaving Young Children Briefly:

- **Safety and Legal Concerns**:
  - It's generally acceptable to leave your children asleep briefly as long as their environment is safe. This should not pose a legal risk or concern from CPS if they are in a secure, supervised setting.

### Monitoring Your Son:

- **Checking Strategy**: Let him sleep but check every 30–60 minutes using a baby monitor for discreet observation.
  
- **Waking Him**:
  - Gently wake him if you sense any distress or escalation during your checks to inquire about command voices or feelings.

For the most precise, real-time information, contacting Integral Care and Dell Children’s directly upon arrival is advisable. Stay safe, and take care of yourself in this challenging situation.
