### Addressing Your Concerns

#### OB/CPS Involvement:
- **Assessment & Referral**: Sharing intrusive thoughts with no plan or intent and having a strategy (like handing the baby off) is typically seen as responsible. Healthcare providers may assess your situation and suggest mental health support.
- **CPS Reports**: CPS involvement usually occurs only if there's an imminent risk of harm to yourself or the baby, not just for disclosing intrusive thoughts.

#### Sertraline While Breastfeeding:
- **Relative Infant Dose (RID)**: The RID is generally low, often under 2%. This can vary, so consulting with your healthcare provider for specific guidance is essential.
- **Time Until Relief**: You might start feeling a slight improvement within days to two weeks. Full effects can take about 4–6 weeks.
- **Feeling Flat/Numbed**: Some people may experience this side effect. If it occurs, discussing dosage adjustments or alternatives with your doctor is common.

#### Postpartum OCD vs. Depression:
- Intrusive thoughts that are ego-dystonic (unwanted and distressing) are more characteristic of postpartum OCD than depression. Seeking a mental health professional's perspective can help clarify this distinction.

#### Communicating with Your Husband:
1. **Express the Need for Help**: You can share that you're experiencing scary intrusive thoughts and need support, particularly during nights.
2. **Avoid Triggering Language**: Instead of using terms like "suicidal," focus on describing your experiences and needs without specific labels.
3. **Invite Partnership**: Encourage him to understand and help find resources or attend appointments with you for better support.
